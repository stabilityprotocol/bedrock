Inline Emphasis — Tier 1
One keyword per section heading accented with three-way contrast: serif italic + pink (#C026A0) inside an IBM Plex Sans heading.
Seeing is no longer enough.
Proof at the moment of creation.
Built for where certainty matters most.
From capture to verified in three steps.
Those who stand behind the integrity of data.
Usage Rules
  • Max 1 accented keyword per heading
  • Used in all 5 numbered section headings (01–05)
  • Never in body copy, captions, or step text
  • Always include fontStyle: italic
  • Always include fontFamily: Source Serif 4
  • Color is always #C026A0

⚠ Never use variant="h1" on inner pages — it triggers Source Serif 4
Never Do
  • ✕  Source Serif 4 on page titles, nav, forms, inner-page h1
  • ✕  Inter for section headings or nav items (use IBM Plex Sans)
  • ✕  Inter for hashes, IDs, evidence data (use IBM Plex Mono)
  • ✕  Hardcode "'Inter', sans-serif" — import from fontTokens
  • ✕  Pink italic emphasis inside body copy, captions, or step text
  • ✕  More than one Tier 1 accent per heading
  • ✕  Inventing a fifth font — only these four are loaded
  • ✕  Loading extra weights beyond the declared subset

Design Principles

🔒
Trust & Security
Every surface communicates reliability. Dark, high-contrast interfaces that convey confidentiality and control.
⚡
Technology-Forward
Modern, clean layouts with precise type and deliberate use of color. Innovation without distraction.
♿
Accessibility First
No pure black or white. High-contrast foreground text. Visible focus rings on every interactive element.
🎯
Purposeful Emphasis
Pink Accent and gradients are reserved for moments that demand attention — never used decoratively.
