# Background

Stability Solutions was the industry sponsor for a capstone project for Computing & Law students from Singapore Management University. [BedRock](http://bedrockapp.space/) is the outcome of the project. The project members were Ranveer Bains, Nicole Michaella Daduya, Sarah Do, Vaibhav Mittal, S Bharath Raj, and John Shutler. In accordance with capstone project requirements, the BedRock source code was turned over to Stability upon completion of the project. We are pleased to release the source code for public access and research, and with the hope that scalable verification technologies will benefit society as we enter an AI-centric era.

## Challenge

In a world where AI has made data generation cheap and infinite, how do we verify the integrity of data? In relation to video files in particular, and in the context of the intersection of computing and law, how do we verify whether they have been tampered and whether they can be relied upon as electronic evidence?

## Response

The project members developed BedRock as a response to this challenge. By chunking and recursively hashing video in real time as it’s being recorded, and anchoring the hashes to Stability’s Global Trust Network, a cryptoless public blockchain, anyone can subsequently (i) verify the integrity of the video in its entirety; and (ii) if tampered, identify from which chunk onwards the video was tampered with.

## Modalities

The project members showcased BedRock along 3 modalities: web app, phone app, and CCTV. BedRock can easily be adapted for verifying other video modalities including robots/drones, police bodycams, and more.
